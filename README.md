# Magnum Auto

Magnum Auto is a Dubai-based wholesale supplier of genuine auto parts and lubricants.

We help automotive wholesalers, importers, distributors, dealers and service networks source parts from a wide range of brands. Our team supports every order from enquiry and quotation through consolidation, export and delivery worldwide.

With more than 12 years of industry experience, Magnum Auto is focused on reliable supply, clear communication and practical support for international automotive buyers.
